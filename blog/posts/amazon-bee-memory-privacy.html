Amazon Bee: useful memory, with a privacy question

Factual cutoff: October 16, 2026. This is announcement-based reporting; we have not tested these devices.

Amazon's Bee Frontier is a $74.99 wearable that can be worn as a band or clipped to clothing. Bee Eclipse is a magnetic phone attachment announced from $99.99 and coming soon. Amazon says the devices can turn conversations into summaries when switched on.

Basic includes summaries and fourteen-day memory. Suggested to-dos and app connections require Bee Pro, listed at $11.99 monthly or $99.99 annually, with a 30-day trial for new customers. Amazon shopping integration is optional and requires Pro. These are manufacturer-described capabilities, not results independently tested by AI Lab Report.

The everyday appeal is remembering a plan or detail you might otherwise forget. The tradeoff involves other people too: a microphone button and indicator light do not establish that everyone has given permission. Amazon says audio is deleted after processing, while encrypted transcripts and summaries can remain.

Would that convenience justify wearing or carrying a listening device? The Short preserves that question for viewers rather than making a product endorsement.
